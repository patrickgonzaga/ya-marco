How it's made

Three steps. One very hot kitchen.

  1. 1

    Fry the garlic

    Fresh garlic goes into hot oil and gets stirred until it turns golden and crisp.

  2. 2

    Bring the heat

    Chilies join the pan and cook low and slow, stirred by hand the whole time.

  3. 3

    Fill & seal

    Every jar is filled while fresh, capped, and sealed with a thank-you sticker.
